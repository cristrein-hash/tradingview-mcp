## Reading lens (how value-area is used)
- **ACCEPTING_ABOVE_VALUE** = price has built/left value and the developing session is trading above its own POC. In a macro-bear context this is the single most informative "change-of-character" tell: the auction is no longer being rejected back into prior value; it is accepting higher. Supply ahead becomes potential *fuel* (shorts trapped above value), not a wall — provided dist_poc is meaningfully positive (price not just grazing the top of value).
- **IN_VALUE** (dist_poc ≈ 0) = price is pinned inside the developing value area, balanced, undecided. The auction has NOT resolved. Whatever the form looks like (BOS chains, NAS LONG), the value read says "no acceptance yet" → base is incomplete, the move is unproven. In a bear this is where bear-pullback-traps and incomplete-base-absorptions live.
- **BELOW_VALUE_REJECTED / dist_poc negative + ACCEPTED_BELOW_VALUE tpo** = price is being held below the developing value / accepted below it. Distribution, not absorption. In a bear this is the cleanest "supply-as-wall / honest-residual" signature.
- **tpo_acc** cross-checks svp_state: ACCEPTED_ABOVE vs INSIDE vs ACCEPTED_BELOW tells whether the *time*-based acceptance agrees with the *state*. Disagreement (e.g. ACCEPTING_ABOVE_VALUE state but INSIDE tpo) = acceptance is fresh/fragile, not consolidated.

**Conclusion:** trap-vs-genuine is governed by *whether price accepts above its own developing value*, not by the weekly slope. Weekly-negative + ACCEPTING_ABOVE_VALUE (with real dist_poc) = washout/CoC; weekly-negative + IN_VALUE/below-POC + ACCEPTED_BELOW = trap/wall.
