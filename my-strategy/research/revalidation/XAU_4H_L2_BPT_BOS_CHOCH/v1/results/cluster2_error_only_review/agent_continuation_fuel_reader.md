## ANSWER — Is there a sub-structure inside IN_VALUE that separates base from distribution?

YES — and it is consistent across the four genuinely in-value episodes (5627, 1522, 3825, 3929).
The separator is a **conjunction**, not IN_VALUE alone:

1. **Floor/ceiling geometry**: BASE wants demand-near + supply-far (1522: dem 0.77 / sup 2.4 FAR).
   DISTRIBUTION shows supply-near or demand-absent/remote (5627: dem 10.57 away; 3825: both <0.6
   pinned; 3929: symmetric, no edge). IN_VALUE with the buyer floor close and the seller wall remote
   = base; IN_VALUE with the floor missing/far and a wall overhead = trap.

2. **Effort signature (bubbles)**: BASE prints NET BUY effort or at least no sell wall (1522 buy=2,
   sell=0). DISTRIBUTION prints one-sided SELL bubbles (5627=15, 3929=10, 4401=11). Sell-bubble count
   is the single cleanest wall-vs-fuel discriminator in this set.

3. **OHLC sub-structure**: BASE = washout-that-develops — sweep the low and CLOSE NEAR THE HIGH, then
   HOLD (1522: L1857 C1876, then acceptance). DISTRIBUTION = thrust-that-fails — spike up and give back
   to/under POC (5627 rejection wick; 3825 fade off 1847; 3929/3949/4401 markup INTO the ceiling with
   entry at the top of the run). "Compression that develops" (holding after a reclaim) is bullish;
   "markup that decays" (rejection back to POC) is not.

So IN_VALUE is **not** ambiguous once you read the sub-structure. The wall is "POC + supply-near/
floor-absent + one-sided sell bubbles + thrust-rejected." The accumulated fuel is "POC + demand-near/
supply-far + net buy effort + low-sweep-and-hold." 1522 is the clean fuel print; the rest of the set
fails one or more of the three legs.

**Common variable in the weak/refuted set**: each pairs IN_VALUE/above-value with the WRONG
geometry — either a near or far-demand-absent floor plus one-sided SELL bubbles, or price already
chased ABOVE the POC into a sell shelf (3949, 4401). The residue is not "missing variable"; it is the
**same continuation/fuel weakness as Cluster 1** — IN_VALUE/below-POC was read as a base when the
floor effort and the bubble effort both said the markup was being walled, not fueled.
