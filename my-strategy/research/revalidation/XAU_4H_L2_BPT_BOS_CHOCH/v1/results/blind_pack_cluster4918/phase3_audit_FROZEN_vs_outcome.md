### Did the discriminator axis (weekly → RSI → form → demand/supply) hold against reality?
- **Weekly sign (Camada 1) — held strongly.** It correctly inverted the meaning of the identical FLUSH_V surface: 1661 (weekly −0.22) trap → loser; 4918 (weekly +0.54 + turn) fundo → monumental. Same surface, opposite outcome, separated by weekly. This is the load-bearing lens and it earned its place.
- **RSI position-in-leg — held strongly as a positive discriminator.** 8878 (RSI 56, "room") → monumental vs 8923 (RSI 84, "stretched/climax") → crash, ten days apart on the SAME leg. The RSI-relative lens cleanly split the healthy markup from the climax. Best validated lens after weekly.

- **Demand/supply (SUPPORTED vs FLOATING; supply proximity) — MIXED.** FLOATING_NO_BASE worked beautifully at 8923 (no base → crash into the void, as predicted). But **supply-proximity failed at 4926**: dist_supply=1.61ATR was read as a rejection brake when it was absorbed into a monumental. The demand/supply lens is reliable for *base-presence* (FLOATING vs SUPPORTED) but **unreliable for supply-as-obstacle when momentum is fresh and strong.** This is the one axis component that misfired.

### Were the declared ambiguities (5701, 8923) really hard, or was recoverable signal lost?
- **8923 — genuinely hard, and honest.** The Reader predicted a violent bidirectional move with a sharp correction toward demand because there was no base; that is exactly what happened (spike to 4059, crash to 3944). Not luck — the FLOATING/RSI-84/red-bar signature is real and it fired. If anything the Reader UNDER-rated his own signal (could have leaned exhaustion harder).
- **5701 — hard, but a recoverable tie-breaker was left on the table.** 14 bars of range genuinely looked like absorption, so the ambiguity was honest. BUT the recoverable signal was the regime: bottom_turn=True under NEGATIVE weekly (5701) is not the same as bottom_turn under POSITIVE weekly (4918). The Reader saw this ("Camada 1 bear puxa pra trap") but chose not to let weekly break the tie. Letting Camada 1 dominate when it is unambiguous would have demoted the absorption lean to trap and matched the loser outcome. Partially recoverable.

### Lessons for the lens library (diagnostic — NO gate/score)
1. **bottom_turn is regime-conditioned.** bottom_turn=True only upgrades "trap" to "absorption/bottom" when weekly AGREES (positive or turning). Under bear weekly + cascade −3, bottom_turn alone is unconfirmed → keep the trap/range-top prior. (From 5701.)
2. **Supply proximity is momentum-conditioned (the clearest new lens).** Nearby supply (≈1.6ATR) is a rejection signal ONLY when momentum is fading or the leg is mature/stretched (8923). On a FRESH, strong reclaim impulse (4926, riding the 4918 turn), nearby supply is a target to be consumed, not a wall — weight it DOWN, expect absorption. The Reader's biggest single miss came from inverting this. (From 4926 vs 8923.)
3. **Entry-location-within-the-leg deserves a timing penalty, not just a note.** Buying the apex of a completed fast recovery in TRANSITION (cascade −1) is a chase; expect a retest of the pullback low before continuation. The buyable point is the higher-low retest, not the apex. (From 6887.)
4. **A declared ambiguity should still let the unambiguous Camada-1 factor break the tie.** When form and regime conflict (5701), the Reader's instinct to not force is right, but the tie should default to the dominant unambiguous axis (weekly sign), not to the softer surface read.
5. **RSI-position and weekly-sign are the two lenses that earned promotion to "load-bearing."** Both inverted identical surfaces correctly and matched outcomes. The demand/supply lens is reliable for base-PRESENCE but not for supply-as-obstacle.

### Honesty check (luck vs read)
- 4918 and 8878 monumentals were high-confidence, multi-factor convergent reads, not luck — the same surface produced losers elsewhere (1661, 5701) and the Reader's discriminators are what separated them. Real lift over surface.
- 8923's success was the Reader's *feared branch* firing exactly as described — a genuine read of a real signature, not hindsight.
- 4926's monumental was captured by the trade but the Reader's NATURE label (rejection-risk) leaned wrong; calling it a "good read" would be hindsight-flattering. It is an honest miss with a correct backbone.
- 5701 and 6887 are honest losers where the Reader either correctly declared ambiguity (5701) or explicitly pre-flagged the exact failure mode and then read through it (6887).

**Bottom line (no edge claim, no promotion):** The weekly-sign and RSI-position lenses demonstrably lifted reading quality above the surface (which would have lumped 1661 with 4918, and 8878 with 8923, into one bucket). The single recurring blind spot is **supply-as-obstacle under fresh strong momentum** (4926), and a secondary one is **entry-at-recovery-apex timing** (6887). Both are concrete, addable lenses — diagnostic only.
