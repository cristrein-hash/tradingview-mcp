## Reading lens (how I read supply-as-WALL vs supply-BEING-CONSUMED)

Before the episodes, the discriminators I apply:

- **Supply is a WALL when**: price stalls under it (upper wicks rejecting, closes failing to extend), supply is *fresh/untested* and above with clean sky into it (nothing has eaten it yet), and the approach is a *first* tag with no prior acceptance above value. The structure is asking permission it hasn't earned.
- **Supply is BEING CONSUMED (fuel/target) when**: candles close THROUGH prior supply with body (not wick), value-area is migrating up underneath the move (POC/VAH dragging higher), bubbles/NAS confirm the impulse is the *second* leg of a move that already broke structure (BOS chain), and dist_poc-above-value is paired with *expanding* range bars rather than a single exhaustion spike. Acceptance above value + a BOS chain reframes near supply as the next liquidity pool to run, not a ceiling.
- **dist_poc-above-value**: healthy expansion when value is *catching up* (POC rising, multiple closes holding above VAH = ACCEPTING_ABOVE_VALUE earned by acceptance, not a single spike). Exhaustion when the displacement is one parabolic bar with RSI saturated AND no value migration underneath — value got left behind and isn't following.

## Answers to the packet's LIVE questions

- **What makes the 2nd sibling (4926) look dangerous, and is the danger real?** It looks dangerous because of *static near-field flags read in isolation*: SUPPLY_BLOCKS + clean_sky=False (supply 1.61ATR overhead), dist_poc 2.07ATR above value (looks over-extended), and late contrarian NAS→SHORT / sell-bubbles. **The danger is largely NOT real.** Read as a trajectory: the regime *improved* between sibling-1 (4918) and sibling-2 (4926) — cascade flipped -1→+1, macro_broken resolved — the form went from compression to a +13 body expansion bar, value MIGRATED up +14pts (VAH 1814→1829) to follow the move, and RSI sits mid-range (52) with headroom. That is supply-BEING-CONSUMED (the BOS-chain second impulse driving into the next liquidity pool), not supply-as-WALL. The "danger" is an artifact of judging the second impulse as an isolated snapshot against a near-supply gate, instead of as the continuation of a move whose value is migrating up. The late NAS/bubble SHORTs are the normal contrarian noise that fires at any extension and should not get veto priority over an aligned, value-confirmed second impulse.
- **dist_poc large in bull = expansion or exhaustion?** Expansion **when value migrates to follow** (POC/VAH rising, multiple closes accepting above) and RSI has room — 4926, 8940. Exhaustion when displacement is one spike with saturated RSI and value left behind (no migration) — the 8923 RSI-82 case is the honest residual.
- **Does ACCEPTING_ABOVE_VALUE change the read of nearby supply?** Yes — in an *up* cascade it converts nearby supply from wall to target/fuel (4926). In a *down* cascade it does NOT (1661 — acceptance above value + near supply is just a counter-trend bounce into a wall). Cascade direction gates the interpretation.
- **Near supply: wall or target/fuel?** Target/fuel when there's an aligned second impulse with body-close BOS chain and value migrating up (4926). Wall when price is rejected off it, compresses back onto POC, prints EQH, with no value migration (8878).
- **Does the Reader treat the 2nd impulse as an isolated trade?** It should NOT — and the failure mode is exactly that. 4926 only reads correctly as a *continuation of 4918's origin*: the pair tells the story (origin compression → markup expansion) that neither bar tells alone.
- **Should structural continuation get interpretive priority over a supply-wall flag?** Yes, when the continuation is *earned* — regime aligned up + value migrating + body-close BOS chain + RSI headroom (4926, 8940). The supply-wall flag should only win when the local tape actually confirms the wall (rejection + EQH + compression, as in 8878). Continuation priority is conditional on trajectory evidence, not automatic — which keeps it a *reading*, not a gate.
