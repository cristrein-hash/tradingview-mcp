## METHOD OF ADJUDICATION (read before the verdicts)

The two readers agree on every static fact. They split only on **interpretation of value-area
migration relative to price displacement**. So the adjudication cannot be "who has more flags" —
it must name the single variable that, when its sign flips, flips the read from
supply-being-consumed to supply-as-wall WITHOUT becoming a threshold/gate.

**The candidate arbitral variable I isolate — VALUE-MIGRATION PHASE (lead vs lag):**

- Value LEADS / rises UNDER the move (POC & VAH dragging up *beneath* price while bodies close
  through, range still expanding, RSI mid) = supply is being CONSUMED; value is building a floor
  the move stands on; nearby supply = the next target/fuel.
- Value LAGS / rises INTO an already-spent move (POC & VAH catching up *to* a price that has
  stopped extending — give-back close, upper-wick, range contracting, dist_poc stretched while
  the last bar fails to make a new body-high) = supply is a WALL; "acceptance" is value reaching
  a price that is no longer being pushed.

This is not a number. `dist_poc` large, `ACCEPTING_ABOVE_VALUE`, and "VAH migrated +Npts" are
IDENTICAL in both phases — which is exactly why both steelmen could weaponize the same fact. The
discriminator is the **phase relationship**: is the most recent completed bar still *extending*
the displacement (lead/consumption), or has it stopped extending while value catches up (lag/wall).
The corroborating triad that disambiguates the phase without gating it:
**(1) last-bar body vs give-back** (does the entry bar close at/near its own high, or off it),
**(2) supply transition between siblings/recent bars** (FAR→NEAR/BLOCKS = the ceiling came to
the price, a lag tell), **(3) RSI headroom + divergence presence** (mid/rising-with-room = lead;
saturated or div-flipped = lag).

Cascade direction is the **pre-condition** (up-cascade is required for either read to even be a
continuation question — secondary 1661/5701 confirm: same VA state in a down-cascade is just a
counter-trend wall-bounce). Cascade does NOT adjudicate consumption-vs-wall *inside* an up-cascade;
value-migration phase does.

## SINGLE CANDIDATE ARBITRAL VARIABLE (supply-consumption vs supply-as-wall, NON-gate)

**VALUE-MIGRATION PHASE — lead vs lag** — whether the value area (POC/VAH) is rising UNDER the
move while the most-recent completed bar is still EXTENDING the displacement (body close at/near
its high) = supply being CONSUMED; versus the value area rising INTO an already-spent move whose
most-recent bar has STOPPED extending (give-back close / upper-wick / EQH) while a near ceiling
has just arrived = supply as WALL.

It is a *reading*, not a gate, because the same scalar facts (`dist_poc` large,
`ACCEPTING_ABOVE_VALUE`, "VAH migrated +Npts", supply near) appear in BOTH phases — the variable
is the temporal RELATIONSHIP (does value lead under, or lag into) read off the trajectory and the
entry bar, not any threshold on those scalars. Cascade-up is the pre-condition that makes the
question a continuation question at all (down-cascade = counter-trend bounce, per 1661/5701);
phase is what adjudicates inside an up-cascade.
