## Synthesis (skeptic side)

- **Distinguishing supply-as-WALL from supply-BEING-CONSUMED without a gate**: the wall reads
  as a wall only when it CO-OCCURS with (a) supply pulled NEAR (≤~1.6ATR, clean_sky False),
  (b) dist_poc stretched (>~2ATR) OR a fresh rejection/EQH cap at the ceiling, and (c) effort
  fading (sell bubbles dominant / NAS rolling short / RSI div absent). When supply is FAR/absent
  and effort is heavy (buy bubbles, BOS continuation), the same NAS-SHORT/RSI reading is just a
  lower-TF oscillator fighting an intact backbone — supply is the TARGET/fuel, not the wall.
- **Across the 5 primaries only ONE earns a genuine wall (4926)**, one is borderline
  (8878, compression-under-near-supply with rejection+EQH), and three (4918, 8940, 6887) deny a
  wall outright — clean sky / supply far / price at-or-below POC / buyer effort present.
- **dist_poc large in bull**: by itself it is NOT exhaustion. It only turns dangerous when
  paired with a NEAR wall (4926). At 8940 the large/healthy expansion has clean sky — saludable.
- **ACCEPTING_ABOVE_VALUE**: does NOT automatically protect the trade — at 4926 it reads as
  "value chased up to a spent move under near supply," which is the bearish interpretation.

## Where the fade case is WEAKEST (honesty)
- **4918, 8940, 6887**: no wall to fade — fading them requires inventing supply that the OB and
  clean_sky data say isn't there. 8940 especially (clean sky + 13 buy bubbles + steepest slope)
  is an expansion the skeptic cannot honestly short.
- **8878**: the wall (0.59ATR + EQH + rejection) is real, but cascade=3 BULL + buy bubbles 4 +
  triple BOS means it may be supply-being-consumed; the fade is contestable, not clean.
- The ONLY clean honest fade is **4926** — and even there the bull weekly_slope is positive, so
  it's a tactical late-chase fade inside a structurally up market, not a trend-reversal fade.
